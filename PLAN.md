# Project status

## Current priority

Economy and active crew management come first, then mission rewards, then story. See [FUNDAMENTALS.md](FUNDAMENTALS.md) for the implemented foundation pass, progression rules, acceptance checks and remaining debt. New narrative expansion is secondary to making gathering, crafting, equipment and upgrade decisions rewarding.

## Planned extensions

These remain design work rather than current gameplay:

- Escort jobs, specimen recovery, recruit rescues and negotiation built on the saved expedition lifecycle.
- Personal ambitions, faction relationships and story threads responding to expedition events and reanimation.

- Playable family-reunion destinations beyond the correspondence and farewell scenes.
- More conversations between companions and reactions to reanimation or class choices.
- Negotiated departure dates, off-site ally relationships and responses to repeatedly postponed farewells.
- Physical changes to the house as borrowed rooms are returned.
- More varied regional resolutions, including negotiation, relocation, testimony and vigils.
