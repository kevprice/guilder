# Mara

Mara is a small brass golem, built and raised by Halden outside the severed guild. Halden taught her repairs and gave her the freedom to choose her own company. After Halden’s death, she followed their treatment notes, brought their letters and brass guild token to the entrance, and restored the crossing.

She chooses to become Rowan’s familiar. Her blue chest light sits beside Halden’s token; a dry compartment beneath her breastplate protects their correspondence. She waits beside the entrance with her tools and kettle. Players can approach her and press E to speak; her conversation changes when the gate opens and after Rowan leaves the estate.

Mara handles deliveries, supplies, repair workers and correspondence. She can cross the boundary that holds Rowan. She is a permanent caretaker rather than a controllable resident or expedition recruit. Her companionship is voluntary, and Rowan’s eventual freedom does not end it.

Existing saved prose updates her former human description to this identity without changing campaign progress.
