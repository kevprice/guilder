# Story and cast reference

The current narrative is documented in [CAMPAIGN.md](CAMPAIGN.md). Individual scenes and mechanical choices are sourced from the game data and regenerated through `npm run docs`.

- [Characters](CHARACTERS.md): all playable people and supporting witnesses.
- [Resident discoveries](RESIDENT_DISCOVERIES.md): where each person is found and what releases them.
- [Homecomings](HOMECOMINGS.md): personal records, destinations and farewells.
- [Room records](PERSONAL_RECORDS.md): diaries and testimony uncovered during exploration.
- [Classes and skills](CLASSES_AND_SKILLS.md): progression, choices and concrete uses.
- [Project status](PLAN.md): implemented systems and planned extensions.

The central question is **When does saving something become refusing to let it live?** Rowan's binding, the old guild's acquired rooms, its preserved companions and the Guest's promise all explore that question through different lives.

Every companion begins as a person trapped inside the guild. Each record adds a specific discovery or relationship. Homecomings can change the party permanently. Story text describes events inside the world; interface text describes actions and meaningful outcomes. Gold and XP are announced when earned.
