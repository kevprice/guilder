# Campaign: The Bound House

Author knowledge and ending spoilers. This is the current canon; generated character and diary references provide the individual scenes.

## Central question

**When does saving something become refusing to let it live?**

The guild created connected doors so distance would not prevent rescue, medicine or reunion. Its house became a home for people who lacked one. Over time its leaders treated connected rooms, resources and people as possessions. Rowan's freedom and the companions' homecomings test whether the rebuilt guild can respect a departure.

## Before the severance

Rowan joined because they wanted a secure place at a table. Halden, the guild surgeon, became someone they loved. They planned a journey without a contract or destination. Rowan repeatedly postponed it to take on more guild work.

Rowan catalogued acquired rooms and accepted the guild's ownership claims. A family's kitchen became Provisioning Room Four; its owner's letter was left out of the catalogue. Personal records make this complicity specific. Other witnesses can contradict Rowan's diary.

Commander Vey had seen help arrive too late and wanted nobody beyond reach. The first doors saved lives. Vey gradually treated refusal as an obstacle to rescue. Ada Merrow withheld repeatable failures from the public report. Vey suspended the experiment, then restarted it after a reassuring test. The suspension reached the study; it was later countermanded. Vey’s ultimate fate remains unconfirmed.

Brass fittings maintain the spell connecting a guild doorway to a distant interior. Recovering the appropriate fitting and restoring its supports lets that interior reconnect. Each doorway has owners, occupants and a history; room connections carry obligations as well as opportunities.

## The collapse

The joining reached the Guest, an intelligence that learns voices and habits from traces in connected places. It offered preservation and reunion. It wants to draw the connected world into one enduring interior where separation becomes impossible.

During the evacuation, Oren braced a collapsing stair with stone-skin; Maelin froze herself and the bell after the alarm began repeating falsely; Ysra became caught in the living map she used to guide people out. Other guild members were held in protective wards, reflected doors, roots, pressure seals and signal echoes.

Rowan was mortally injured helping people on the stair. Halden saved them through an emergency binding to the house. The survivors severed the dangerous connections. The Guest's crossing stopped, rooms were scattered, and Rowan remained inside.

Thirty years passed outside while Rowan remained conscious inside the surviving house and the companions were held in their wards. Halden searched for a treatment for twenty-nine years, adopted Mara and continued treating patients. They died last autumn. Mara tells Rowan at the gate and brings their correspondence with the inherited token. Later discoveries let Rowan read particular letters from that packet; those letters were not written inside sealed guild rooms.

## The investigation

1. **Entrance and quarters:** Rowan releases Bryn beneath the workshop door and Nell from a folded escape route. Opening requests provide resources and experience.
2. **Service room:** Bryn opens the sealed wall. Restoration reveals records, a cellar key and Ivo's unfinished protective rune. The ledger points to Wren's Crossing.
3. **Returned landing:** Helping the crossing's keeper returns its fitting and tower/labyrinth keys. Rowan reads the severance warning and Sera Venn's name.
4. **Coast and archive:** Sera's chart leads to the missing archive. Rescue priorities affect what survives. Official accounts conflict with personal testimony.
5. **Greenhouse and observatory:** Edda's seed stock and Elian's observations connect restitution, consent and the search for the infirmary.
6. **Infirmary:** Halden's recording explains the emergency binding and what must replace it. Rowan already knows of Halden’s death; the last letter answers the fear that Halden stopped trying. Rowan chooses the privacy of their personal medical history while the guild remains accountable for its orders.
7. **Replacement:** A consent-based covenant or a mechanical containment lattice can sustain the house independently of Rowan. Class choices and optional companion stories change approaches while both routes remain achievable.
8. **Threshold and gate:** The Guest offers a dinner table with Halden, the dead and people who chose to leave. Its figures repeat old conversations and have lost the ability to want a different future. Rowan closes the shutter and accepts a future that cannot recover the lost years.

Both house endings release Rowan immediately: maintain visiting roads by agreement, or begin returning borrowed rooms. Rowan walks beyond the gate. Nell can accompany them when explicitly invited. The house becomes a place they may return to.

## The companions

All thirty companions belonged to the old guild and are discovered within the house. Their release begins a personal story. Living companions were magically preserved across the severance; Lark, Ves, Fenn, Sorrel, Sal and Tavi are spirits whose deaths are revealed through personal evidence.

Bryn searches for her father and chooses what to build after finding his memorial. Nell follows the shelter routes she once protected. Ivo confronts Ada's failures and develops a practice that permits refusal. Oren learns to belong without holding every structure up. Maelin learns that saving people gave them time, not a guarantee about their lives. Ysra learns to map people and claims, including places deliberately left private.

Every companion has a homecoming destination and a permanent farewell. Some return to living families or new work; some meet descendants; spirits deliver their final answer and pass on. Equipment returns to storage and their histories remain in the register. Postponing the conversation preserves the choice for later.

## The wider guild

Eight regional campaigns recover rooms and field records from the old network: Lantern Fen, Ash March, Glass Coast, Rootwild, White Stair, Clockwork Ward, Undersea Vault and Fallen Star. Present-day local keepers are distinct from the old guild members trapped in those rooms. The sixth job returns a room key; restoring the returned interiors reveals their residents.

The regions concern flood displacement, dangerous industry, deceptive beacons, uncontrolled cultivation, stalled weather, expired emergency authority, disputed wealth and a signal that keeps calling the lost. The playable missions currently provide rescue, investigation and repair; the personal correspondence gives these efforts their human stakes.

## Writing and presentation

Room records use diaries, letters, recipes, maps and testimony to reveal specific lives. Discoveries change knowledge, relationships or available actions. Narration stays inside the world. Interface text explains an action or a real consequence. Reward messages announce gold, XP and items received.

The house is warm, worn and magical. Its prison passages and threats are darker. Important objects have affordances; interaction descriptions belong below the canvas. Characters keep their own positions when control changes.
